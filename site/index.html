02 / UNDER THE HOOD

From pixels to meaning.

01

See at several scales

A ResNet-50 backbone and feature pyramid turn the image into maps that carry information about small and large objects.

02

Look for objects

The region proposal network suggests boxes worth examining. RoI pooling extracts a compact feature for each proposal.

03

Make the call

The trained prediction head assigns a VOC class, refines the box, and gives each detection a confidence score.
